The three problems

parser — the shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant books 7 April and the invite says 4 July. Day and month swap, so it only happens when the day is 12 or less. Every tenant on file writes the day first. This is what #4471 describes, and the queued fix is for this.

hint — booking-v3’s form tells people to type month first. Its date field prints MM/DD/YYYY, people type what it says, and the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The report looks exactly like a parser report: a swapped day and month. It comes only through the form channel of booking-v3 tenants. Fixing the parser does not touch it. Intake’s tag does not tell the two apart; it guesses from the browser, so go by tenant, form and channel (causeOf in src/lib/kb/causes.ts does this).

one-day — the date moves by one day, the month stays right. Tuesday shows as Monday; a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and one in Toronto gets Monday for the same meeting; an invite arrives before the booking; “every booking in June is one day off”. That is a date shifted across a time zone or a DST change, not a swapped day and month. #4471’s table files these under parser by tenant and footnotes them. The parser fix will not move them back.

For the desk

  • Do not close #4471 on the parser fix alone. Check the hint and one-day rows first.
  • A swapped date from a booking-v3 tenant’s form is hint, whatever intake tagged it.
  • A date off by exactly one day is one-day. Do not merge it into a swap ticket because the subject says “wrong day”: subjects match across all three problems.
